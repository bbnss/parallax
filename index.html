The Undisputed Facts: An Influx and an Intervention

Across nearly every source analyzed—from BBC World to TASS—the core sequence of events remains consistent. This massive influx led to immediate humanitarian concerns, notably highlighted by multiple accounts detailing bodies recovered from the water, reaching figures as high as 16 or even 18 deceased individuals according to ANSA and TASS.
